Live demo: the caregiver's phone app and the patient's smartwatch

myDoc has two parts that talk to each other: a phone app for family caregivers and software for the patient's smartwatch. The watch is simulated here, so you can try the whole product without hardware.

Try it in 3 minutes

  1. Watch: tap the watch, then QUICK DEMO: SET UP AS MAMA NKECHI, then "I've written it down".
  2. Watch: tap the gear icon, then PAIR WITH A CAREGIVER. A 6-digit code appears.
  3. Phone app: sign in (judges: the login is in our submission deck) or create an account. Tap + Add a person, type the code, Send request. On the watch tap YES.
  4. Under the watch, press Sugar creeping up: the app shows an AI early warning while every reading is still normal.
  5. Press Start wandering: an urgent alert arrives. Open the person, then Locate, to follow them on the map.
  6. Open the person, then Doctor report, for the PDF with graphs. Press Sugar high, or hold SOS on the watch, for alerts.

On a phone, open the app and the smartwatch in two tabs with the buttons above. On a laptop this page shows both side by side.
