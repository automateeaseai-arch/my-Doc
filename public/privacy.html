myDOC

Privacy notice

Last updated 30 October 2026 · plain language

myDoc is a research prototype and not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat anything. In an emergency, call 112. Blood-sugar estimates from the optical sensor are experimental.

What myDoc is for

myDoc lets a person wearing a smartwatch share their health readings with people they choose (their caregivers), so those caregivers get alerts when something is wrong.

What we keep

  • Caregivers: name, email, phone number, city and how you use myDoc.
  • The person wearing the watch: name, health conditions, emergency contacts, medicine times, readings (blood sugar, blood pressure, heart rate, oxygen), alerts, whether doses were taken, the watch's battery, and its location when it last reported.
  • For alerts on a closed app: a technical address for each phone that turned them on, and that caregiver's alert settings.

We do not keep payment details. There are no adverts and no tracking or analytics tools.

Who can see it

  • The person's own watch.
  • Only caregivers the person accepted on their watch. They can remove any caregiver at any time (watch → Settings → My caregivers).
  • Nobody else. We never sell or share health data with advertisers, insurers or employers.

Where it is stored

  • Accounts and health records: Google Firebase (Google Cloud), protected by sign-in and security rules that let only the watch and its accepted caregivers read a person's record.
  • Alerts to a closed app pass through a small service on Cloudflare. It stores nothing; the alert text is encrypted to each phone.
  • Some settings (units, which person is selected) stay on your own phone.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it. Short summaries are kept for about 10 days of charts; readings and alerts stay until the person deletes their data. Pairing codes expire after 30 minutes.

Your choices

  • Caregiver: Profile → Delete my account and data. This removes your account, your details and your access to everyone you look after.
  • Person wearing the watch: watch → Settings → Delete my data. This erases your readings, medicine, alerts and location for good, and your caregivers lose access.
  • Stop alerts on a phone: Profile → Alerts when myDoc is closed.
  • Stop monitoring someone: Profile → their name → Stop monitoring.

Research

myDoc is being developed for further study. Data from this app is not used for research. Any future study will ask for separate, informed consent and will be approved by an ethics committee first.

Questions

Email the myDoc team: mydoc.health.team@gmail.com. You can also ask us to delete your data this way. We will update this notice when myDoc changes and show the new date at the top.
